## What this is

An **internal** tool for cyber-fraud investigating officers **after** a complaint is known on **1930 / NCRP / CFCFRMS**.

It helps officers: follow money hops · spot reused mule accounts · store evidence · draft BNSS notices · track deadlines.

It does **not** replace citizen complaint channels.

## The ask (small · time-boxed)

Authorise a **4–6 week closed-case pilot**:

1. **5–10** closed / redacted cyber-fraud cases + hop sheets  
2. **One champion IO** + **one supervisor**  
3. **Legal cell** review of BNSS notice wording  

**Success:** time-to-usable trail vs current Excel process → then decide staging, gov email, bank/NCRP next steps.
